# Genre 01: Essential Commerce

**Target Segment:** General small businesses, foundational D2C brands, everyday product merchants.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Clean Product Grid:** Balanced card spacing, minimal visual clutter, high-clarity product cutouts or neutral background photography.
- **Large Search Bar:** Prominent, instant-query input box with predictive autocomplete and auto-categorization.
- **Category Navigation:** Intuitive horizontal or dropdown category bars with direct hierarchy pathways.
- **Simple Checkout:** One-page or streamlined 2-step checkout flows with standard Apple Pay / Google Pay / 1-click Express buttons.
- **Strong Mobile Layout:** Bottom navigation bars, touch-friendly tap targets, sticky add-to-cart bars.
- **Neutral Branding:** Monochromatic or subtle earth-tone typography and backgrounds that let products take center stage.
