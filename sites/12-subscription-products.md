# Genre 12: Subscription Products

**Target Segment:** Recurring consumable businesses, membership boxes, health & supplement subscriptions, recurring pet supplies, coffee clubs.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Subscription Frequency:** Granular delivery interval selectors ("Deliver every 2, 4, 6, or 8 weeks"), calendar day choices.
- **Recurring Order Explanation:** Clear "Subscribe & Save 15%" vs "One-time purchase" pill toggles, savings per unit breakdown, automated refill timelines.
- **Pause/Skip Controls:** Transparent "Skip a shipment or cancel anytime in 1 click" confidence copy right above the primary CTA button.
- **Membership Benefits:** Exclusive subscriber pricing, free shipping on all recurring orders, access to limited-edition drops, complimentary gift on 3rd renewal.
- **Renewal Information:** Explicit next billing date notifications, automated email pre-alerts 3 days before renewal charge, clear account dashboard management overview.
