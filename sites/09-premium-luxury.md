# Genre 09: Premium Luxury

**Target Segment:** High jewelry, haute horlogerie, heritage fashion houses, bespoke automobiles, fine art, superyachts.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Minimal Navigation:** Hidden drawer menus, ultra-sparing top headers with only 2-3 links, understated iconography.
- **Spacious Layout:** Grand scale white space / negative space, generous line-heights, dramatic architectural margins.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-contrast serif headlines (Didot, Bodoni, Garamond, Caslon) paired with ultra-tracked micro sans-serif labels.
- **High-Quality Imagery:** Full-bleed cinematic 4K photography, dramatic lighting, slow-motion video backdrops, zero visual compression artifacts.
- **Appointment or Inquiry CTA:** "Book an Appointment", "Contact a Client Advisor", "Request Private Viewing", "Bespoke Commission Inquiry", discreet concierge WhatsApp buttons.
