# Genre 02: Fashion Atelier

**Target Segment:** Clothing, apparel, designer boutiques, footwear, fashion accessories.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Editorial Photography:** High-fashion art direction, campaign imagery, full-bleed hero banners, lifestyle video loops.
- **Large Visual Product Cards:** 2:3 or 3:4 aspect ratio cards displaying on-model shots, alternate angle hovers, and video previews.
- **Collection-Based Navigation:** Seasonal drops (e.g., "Fall/Winter '26", "Resort", "Capsule 04"), thematic editorial taxonomy.
- **Size and Color Variants:** Instant visual swatch circles, fit/cut selectors, stock status per variant, model measurement indicators.
- **Lookbook Sections:** "Shop the Look" hotspots, interactive lookbooks, styling recommendation carousels.
