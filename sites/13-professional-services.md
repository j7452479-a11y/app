# Genre 13: Professional Services

**Target Segment:** Digital agencies, strategy consultancies, executive coaches, corporate trainers, design studios, professional service firms selling packages.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Service Cards:** Modular cards showcasing specific practice areas, deliverable scope breakdowns, and starting investment figures.
- **Package Comparison:** Side-by-side tier matrices (e.g. Starter Audit vs Full Strategy Sprint vs Enterprise Retainer) with feature checkmarks.
- **Inquiry Forms:** Multi-step intake questionnaires (project budget, timeline, company stage, key challenges) that filter qualified leads.
- **Appointment CTA:** Embedded Calendly/ChiliPiper booking widgets ("Schedule a 30-Minute Discovery Call"), sticky consultation triggers.
- **Testimonials and Case Studies:** Quantitative client impact metrics ("+140% conversion increase", "$35M ARR unlocked"), video founder testimonials, logo social proof bars.
