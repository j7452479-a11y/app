# Genre 11: B2B Catalog

**Target Segment:** Wholesalers, industrial distributors, raw material manufacturers, commercial kitchen suppliers, contract packaging, custom fabricators.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Quote-Request Workflow:** "Request a Custom Quote (RFQ)", Bill-of-Materials (BOM) Excel/CSV upload tool, dynamic cart-to-quote conversion button.
- **Bulk Pricing:** Tiered quantity discount matrices (e.g. 1-9: $14.50, 10-49: $12.20, 50-99: $9.80, 100+: Contact Sales), minimum order quantities (MOQ).
- **Product Specifications:** Rigorous engineering tables (tolerances, hardness, tensile strength, voltage ratings, compliance standards ANSI/ISO/RoHS).
- **Downloadable Documents:** One-click links to PDF technical datasheets, CAD 3D models (STEP/IGES/DWG), safety data sheets (MSDS/SDS), material certifications.
- **Business Customer Forms:** Credit application / Net-30 terms forms, resale tax exemption certificate uploaders, multi-user enterprise purchasing accounts.
