# Genre 05: Home and Furniture

**Target Segment:** Furniture, interior design, lighting, home decor, architectural finishes, bedding and textiles.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Large Product Imagery:** Immersive full-viewport room scenes, 4K texture close-ups of wood grain/marble/fabrics, 3D AR room placers.
- **Room-Based Collections:** "Shop by Room" (Living Room, Dining, Home Office, Bedroom, Outdoor Patio, Entryway) navigation taxonomy.
- **Material and Dimension Sections:** Detailed dimensional blueprints with width × depth × height diagrams, doorway clearance requirements, fabric swatch ordering kits.
- **Delivery Information:** White-glove in-room assembly callouts, flat-pack box dimensions, freight freight-tier calculators, postal delivery ETA estimates.
- **Gallery-Led Browsing:** User-generated customer interior photos, designer showhouse galleries, interactive tagged room lookbooks.
