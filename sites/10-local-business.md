# Genre 10: Local Business

**Target Segment:** Neighborhood stores, regional bakeries, local indie bookshops, specialty delis, community farm shares, regional artisans.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Location-First Design:** Prominent header bar with address, neighborhood badge ("Greenwich Village", "Capitol Hill", "Bandra West"), interactive map widget, opening hours indicator ("Open Now until 9 PM").
- **WhatsApp/Contact CTA:** Floating click-to-WhatsApp button, direct one-tap telephone dialer (`tel:`), instant SMS inquiry trigger.
- **Delivery Information:** Clear delivery radius perimeter maps (e.g. "Free delivery within 3 miles"), local courier partners, curbside pickup instructions.
- **Local Offers:** "Neighborhood resident 10% off", daily morning specials, lunch rush combo discounts, regional community loyalty programs.
- **Simple Product Ordering:** Rapid 1-page checkout without forced account registration, simplified item lists, quick payment via local rails (UPI, Apple Pay, cash on delivery/pickup).
