# Genre 03: Beauty Studio

**Target Segment:** Cosmetics, skincare, haircare, salons, aesthetic clinics, wellness formulations.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Soft Visual Hierarchy:** Pastel/nude palettes, rounded pill tags, gentle drop shadows, calming typography, clean white space.
- **Product Routines:** "Step 1: Cleanse, Step 2: Treat, Step 3: Hydrate" sequencing, routine builder bundles, AM/PM routine toggles.
- **Ingredient Information:** Interactive ingredient glossary, active percentage callouts (e.g. 10% Niacinamide), clean formulation badges.
- **Before/After Sections:** Interactive before/after split sliders, clinical trial timeline statistics (e.g. "94% noticed firmer skin in 4 weeks").
- **Reviews and Trust Badges:** Dermatologist-tested seals, Leaping Bunny / cruelty-free icons, verified customer review filters with skin-type tags.
