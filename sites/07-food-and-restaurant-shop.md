# Genre 07: Food and Restaurant Shop

**Target Segment:** Fast-casual restaurants, artisanal bakeries, cafes, specialty coffee roasters, packaged gourmet food brands.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Menu Categories:** Sticky horizontal navigation (Starters, Mains, Bowls, Sides, Drinks, Desserts, Catering).
- **Add-ons and Modifiers:** Modal drawer with radio buttons (Base selection: Rice vs Greens), checkboxes for toppings, extra protein price differentials (+$3.50), special instruction notes.
- **Pickup and Delivery Options:** Prominent toggle switcher at page header (Pickup vs Delivery) with auto-address lookup and store branch selector.
- **Time-Slot Selection:** "ASAP" vs "Schedule for Later" with 15-minute incremental booking windows and kitchen cutoff notices.
- **Visual Food Imagery:** Vibrant high-contrast food photography, ingredient cross-sections, steam/sizzle hero loops, macro shots that drive appetite appeal.
