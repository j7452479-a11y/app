# Genre 08: Artisan Market

**Target Segment:** Independent makers, ceramicists, leather crafters, heritage textiles, independent jewelry creators, handmade goods.  
**Key Architectural Traits:**
- **Storytelling Layout:** Long-form narrative blocks, workshop video embeds, essay-style product descriptions detailing process and craft philosophy.
- **Maker Profile Section:** "Meet the Maker" author cards, artisan portrait photos, studio location bios, quote callouts.
- **Product Origin Details:** Raw material provenance (e.g. "Stoneware clay from North Carolina", "Horween chromexcel leather from Chicago"), craft technique certifications.
- **Limited-Stock Labels:** "Batch No. 04 — Only 18 Made", "Hand-thrown to order: 3-4 week lead time", "One-of-a-kind unique piece".
- **Gallery-Style Collections:** Asymmetrical editorial masonry grids, exhibition-style catalog numbering, tactile material detail zooms.
